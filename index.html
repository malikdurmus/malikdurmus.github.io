Preference-Guided Curriculum Discovery for Robust RL

Bachelor's thesis · LMU Munich, Chair of Mobile and Distributed Systems · 2026

Reinforcement learning agents generalize better when they train on well-chosen environments. I used an LLM (Gemini) to compare about 88,000 pairs of rendered levels by difficulty, validated the labels against human judgment, and trained a neural network ensemble that ranks new levels.

I then extended a state-of-the-art curriculum algorithm (Sampling for Learnability) with this difficulty model, new selection rules and an adaptive difficulty target, and evaluated it in about 200 GPU-accelerated training runs with paired statistical tests.

RLHF-Gym: Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback

University software engineering project · LMU Munich · 2023–2024 · Grade 1.0

A complete pipeline that teaches robotic agents in MuJoCo from human preferences instead of a hand-written reward. I led the algorithm work: implementations of PEBBLE and SURF on top of Soft Actor-Critic, a learned reward model with semi-supervised learning and data augmentation, and ensemble-based query selection.

The project includes a web interface where people compare two agent videos, a thread-safe backend that feeds their answers into training, and training jobs on the university Slurm cluster.
